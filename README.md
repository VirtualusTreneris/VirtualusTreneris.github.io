# Virtualus Treneris

Virtualus Treneris ("Virtual Trainer") is a touchscreen kiosk app for the OxyGym gym in Klaipėda, Lithuania.

Gym members walk up to the big screen, pick a workout type, choose a trainer and a workout, and then follow the trainer's training video. The video starts right away, and simple on-screen buttons let them pause, rewind or skip ahead.

Nobody needs to look after the screen. When it's left alone, the app goes back to the start screen by itself: after a few minutes of no use, a few minutes after a video is paused, or shortly after a video ends. Members can't zoom, open menus or leave the app, so the kiosk is always ready for the next person.

Workout types: functional training, abs, stretching and TRX, with videos from the gym's own trainers.
